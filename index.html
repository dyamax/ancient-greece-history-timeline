THE TROJAN WAR

The Trojan War, one of the most legendary conflicts in Greek mythology, is believed to have been sparked by the abduction of Helen, the wife of King Menelaus of Sparta, by Paris, a prince of Troy. Lasting for ten years, the war involved numerous epic battles and strategic maneuvers, ultimately resulting in the fall of Troy due to the cunning deception of the Trojan Horse.

FIRST OLYMPIC GAMES

The ancient Olympic Games were a prestigious athletic competition held in Olympia, Greece, dating back to at least 776 BCE. Initially, the games were a single-day event consisting mainly of running races, but over time, they expanded. These games held significant cultural importance in ancient Greece, with athletes competing in honor of the gods and striving for personal glory and prestige.

GRECO-PERSIAN WARS

The Persian Wars were a series of conflicts between the Greek city-states and the Persian Empire, primarily fought during the 5th century BCE. The wars were sparked by Persian expansion into Greek territory and the Greek city-states' desire to maintain their independence. Notable battles such as the Battle of Marathon, the Battle of Thermopylae, and the Battle of Salamis ultimately resulted in Greek victory.
